# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First standalone distribution of the existing VRChat KJV Bible reader.
- Complete offline 66-book KJV, grouped Testament browser, direct verse/range
  entry, continuous touch-selectable reading, search and private study tools.
- Responsive navigation rail and reading view, theme-aware settings cards,
  accessibility controls, independent dimensions/opacity and readable back.
- Hand manipulation, independent two-hand resizing, view/controller following,
  smoothing, pinning, comfort presets and keyboard/controller shortcuts.
- Shared scripture and owner/delegated study hosting with viewer-local screens.
- Persistent reading/ranges, notes, highlights, bookmarks, settings and history.
- Hosted/personal range transitions keep their mode, actions and anchors in sync.
- Add-to-open-scene installer, one-system guard, Undo and portable prefab.
- Self-contained font, materials and generated sound; original material notices.
- Cristian2737 credit, CC0 original contributions, free downloads/optional support.
